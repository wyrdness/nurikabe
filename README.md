## Overview

The nurikabe is a peculiar yokai from Japanese folklore that appears as an invisible barrier in the paths of nighttime travelers. When a person encounters a nurikabe, they find themselves unable to proceed forward, backward, or to either side—an invisible wall blocks every direction they attempt.

The frustrated traveler may wander in circles or attempt various routes, all to no avail. According to tradition, the only way to bypass a nurikabe is to **tap the lower part of the invisible barrier with a stick**, at which point it vanishes.

While usually depicted as invisible, some artistic representations show the nurikabe as a large, wall-like creature with eyes and sometimes limbs.

## Traditional Folklore

### The Nurikabe Experience

When a traveler encounters a nurikabe at night, they experience:

1. **Sudden obstruction** — An invisible wall appears in their path
2. **All directions blocked** — Cannot proceed forward, backward, left, or right
3. **Confusion and frustration** — May wander in circles attempting to find a way around
4. **Mysterious blockage** — No visible obstacle, yet passage is impossible

### The Solution

According to tradition, **tap the lower part of the invisible barrier with a stick**. When this is done correctly, the nurikabe disappears and the traveler can continue.

### Purpose

The nurikabe serves as:
- Folkloric explanation for getting lost at night
- Supernatural obstacle in travelers' tales
- Lesson in problem-solving and persistence
- Mystery element in nighttime journeys

**Pure folklore**: Entirely folkloric entity with no basis in real phenomena—no modern claims, fantastical nature, and cultural tradition support this interpretation.

## How to Defeat a Nurikabe

### Traditional Method

1. When you find yourself blocked in all directions at night
2. Take a stick or staff
3. **Tap the lower part** of the invisible barrier
4. The nurikabe will disappear
5. Continue on your way

**Note**: The nurikabe is not hostile and does not harm travelers—it merely obstructs. The stick-tapping solution is more puzzle-solving than combat.
